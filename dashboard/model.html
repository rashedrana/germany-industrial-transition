Structured & unstructured data

Structured data

The World Bank WDI is a fully structured statistical panel — published indicators keyed by country and year. The model keeps two shapes: a wide country-year table for the headline measures, and a long indicator table so any WDI series can be sliced by DimIndicator[Category].

Unstructured data

Fully structured source — no free text. WDI publishes numeric indicators only; there are no comments, documents or other unstructured content to process.

How the data was cleaned

  • Three derived indicators (DimIndicator keys 14–16) are computed in data prep exactly as in the paper: industrial employment = labour force × (1 − unemployment rate) × industry employment share; manufacturing VA per industrial worker uses industry employment as a proxy for manufacturing employment and is labelled an approximation.
  • Coverage gaps are real WDI gaps, kept as missing rows rather than filled: Poland’s manufacturing share starts 1995, Japan’s 1994, the US’s 1997, China’s 2004; high-technology exports start 2007.
  • Constant-price manufacturing VA for the US, China and the world exists for 2015 only in this WDI vintage — kept as published, not interpolated.
  • The 183-country 2023 cross-section excludes regional aggregates — it is the research paper’s regression sample; the full 261-row dimension keeps every country and aggregate in the extract.
